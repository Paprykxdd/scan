LIGHTING
Use diffuse, even lighting. Avoid harsh shadows and reflections. Cloudy daylight or a softbox works best.

OBJECT PLACEMENT
Place object on a plain, matte, non-reflective surface. Ensure 360° access to walk around it.
📐
OVERLAP
Each photo should share ~60–80% overlap with adjacent shots. This gives the algorithm enough features to match.
🚫
AVOID
Shiny, transparent, or single-color objects scan poorly. Add powder spray to improve surface texture if needed.
